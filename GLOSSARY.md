# dictio

A personal vocabulary notebook: people collect terms they are learning in a
foreign language, with their translations, and train on them.

## Language

**Glossary**:
A named collection of terms for one language pair, from a first language into a
destination language.
_Avoid_: Dictionary, vocabulary list, deck

**Term**:
A word or expression in the glossary's first language that the person is
learning.
_Avoid_: Word, headword

**Translation**:
A rendering of a term in the glossary's destination language. A term has one or
more.
_Avoid_: Meaning, definition

**Entry**:
A term in a glossary together with its translations, example sentences, note and
tags.
_Avoid_: Word, card, record

**Tag**:
A free-text label on an entry. Tags are shared across all glossaries.
_Avoid_: Category, label
